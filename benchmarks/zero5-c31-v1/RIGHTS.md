# ZERO.5-C3.1 rights boundary

C3.1 inherits C3's CC BY-SA 4.0-derived material. The tracked repository binds
the released view, importer, pack hashes, contract, and result evidence. The
large JSONL view, generated packs, and checkpoints remain local build
artifacts.

Neither the dataset nor any C3.1 checkpoint may be published from this pilot.
A separate rights and attribution review is required before publication.
