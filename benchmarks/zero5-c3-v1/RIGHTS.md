# ZERO.5-C3 rights boundary

Corpus 3 is deterministically derived from exact English Wikipedia revisions
under CC BY-SA 4.0. Claims, cloze statements and answers, and retrieval
passages contain or adapt that covered text.

The context-safe retrieval view keeps exact prefixes from both evidence
passages. Compaction does not change the upstream license or create a new
dataset-level grant.

The repository records lineage and rights evidence. It does not decide whether
training, weights, or outputs are derivative works in any jurisdiction.
Public dataset and checkpoint publication remain disabled pending a separate
rights decision. Local experiment artifacts must retain the collection and
source identities.
